Frequently asked questions

How long does a swap take?
It depends on which provider is selected. ChangeNOW typically completes within 10–15 minutes. Godex tends to take longer, typically 30–45 minutes from initiation depending on network confirmation times.
Do I need to create an account?
No. There is no account, no sign-up, and no personal information required. Just enter your receiving address and swap.
What happens if I send the wrong amount?
If the amount differs significantly from what was quoted, the swap may fail. If you provided a refund address your coins will be returned automatically — this is why we always recommend adding one.
What happens if the swap fails?
If a swap fails your coins are returned to your refund address. If no refund address was provided, contact us with your swap ID and we will assist you.
Is there a minimum or maximum swap amount?
There is a small minimum set by the exchange provider — this is shown on the form. There is no upper limit.
Who processes the swap?
Swaps are routed through ChangeNOW or Godex — established non-custodial exchange providers. We automatically select the best available rate for you.
